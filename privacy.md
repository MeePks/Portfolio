_Last updated: 29 August 2026_

This is a personal website operated by Pikesh Maharjan. I aim to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible.

## Information you provide

If you contact me by email or LinkedIn, the information you send is handled through those services and used to respond to your enquiry. This website does not currently maintain an on-site contact-form database or user accounts.

## Hosting and delivery

The site is published through GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Those providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, timestamps, security events and request logs as part of operating, protecting and delivering the website. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy terms.

## Embedded content and external links

Articles may embed YouTube videos using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain or link to third-party maps, social networks and reference sites. Interacting with third-party content may allow those providers to receive technical information and apply their own privacy policies.

## Advertising

Google AdSense is not active unless the site contains a configured publisher ID and active ad units. If advertising is activated later, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where legally required, an approved consent-management platform must be shown before relevant advertising or tracking technologies are used.

See the [Cookie Policy](/cookies/) for more information.

## Your choices

You may avoid embedded media and external links. You can also configure your browser to restrict cookies or storage, although some third-party features may not work as expected.
