I’m Pikesh Maharjan, a Data Engineer based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. My professional work focuses on ETL, data integration, data quality, Master Data Management, SQL, Python, Azure and Databricks.

This website has two separate purposes:

- The [portfolio](/) documents my professional background, selected technical work, education and certifications.
- The [blog](/blog/) is an independent publishing space for original travel notes, practical guides, cultural observations and supporting videos.

## Editorial approach

Articles are based on personal experience and research. When a post includes changing details—such as travel costs, transport options or festival dates—I aim to state when the information was checked and link to useful sources where appropriate.

Cultural articles should distinguish personal observation from historical claims and respect local customs, privacy and photography restrictions. Corrections can be sent through the [contact page](/contact/).

## About the technology

The website is maintained in a public GitHub repository and published through GitHub Pages, with Cloudflare providing the public domain, HTTPS and delivery services.
