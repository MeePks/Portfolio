## Current use

The core portfolio and blog do not require an account and do not intentionally set first-party analytics or advertising cookies. Infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare may use essential security or delivery mechanisms.

## Embedded services

A page containing an embedded YouTube video may connect to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain. YouTube may use cookies, local storage or similar technology when a visitor interacts with the video. External websites opened from this site operate under their own policies.

## Advertising and consent

Advertising placeholders do not load Google advertisements by themselves. Before Google AdSense is activated, this website should implement a Google-certified consent-management platform for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland where required.

The consent interface should allow visitors to make an informed choice before non-essential advertising storage is used and should provide a way to revisit that choice.

## Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to delete or block cookies and site storage. Blocking them may affect embedded or third-party functionality.

For related information, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
